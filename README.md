The Gilded Hour is a top-down heist game for the browser, in vanilla JavaScript with no build step and no network calls. One thief, two jobs — the Meridian Bank at night and the Aurelia Gallery after opening night. Guards patrol with real raycast vision cones that desks, pillars and display cases cut into shadow; wall cameras sweep their arcs and feed the same alarm meter the guards fill. Read the patrols, sneak or sprint (sprinting is loud), throw decoy coins to pull a guard off his round, spend an EMP charge to black out the cameras, and hold F in the open to pick the vault lock while the meter climbs. Every piece of loot slows the bag a little. Meet the quota and reach the van — or don't meet it and watch the driver shake his head. If the alarm trips you get 45 seconds of red light to be gone, and a ghost bonus if it never sings at all.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
